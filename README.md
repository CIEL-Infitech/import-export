# TradeCraft Academy - Import & Export Business Website

A complete, modern interactive web platform designed specifically for teaching the import and export business.

## 📄 Site Structure & Pages

### 1. Home Page (`HomePage.jsx`)
- **Attractive heading**: *Start Your Import & Export Business Journey.*
- **Introduction to import and export**: Explanation of cross-border trade, $32T global market, and how merchant trading works.
- **What students will learn**: 6 core pillars (Business setup & licensing, Product sourcing, Incoterms 2020 & costing, Letters of Credit, Shipping logistics & customs, Finding overseas buyers).
- **Mentorship programmes**: Foundation Cohort, Masterclass & Deal Incubation, Executive Private Mentorship.
- **Benefits of joining**: Legal templates, verified certificates, active practitioner mentorship, risk-free payment structures.
- **Success stories**: Testimonials from alumni with verified shipment metrics.
- **Contact details**: Phone numbers, email, physical office address.
- **Register Now button**: Direct CTA linking to the registration page.

### 2. Registration Page (`RegisterPage.jsx`)
Collects all required fields:
- **Full name**
- **Email address**
- **Mobile number** (with WhatsApp support)
- **Location** (City / Country)
- **Current occupation**
- **Are you a beginner or already in the import/export business?** (Beginner / In Business / Experienced)
- **What are you interested in learning?** (Export procedures, Import clearance, Sourcing & buyers, Incoterms, Letters of Credit, Full program)
- **Password** (for student portal account creation)
- Instant confirmation screen with generated Student ID and orientation kit delivery.

### 3. Other Important Pages
- **About Us (`AboutPage.jsx`)**: Mission, vision, core values, and why practical trade training matters.
- **Courses (`CoursesPage.jsx`)**: In-depth explanation of all training programmes, syllabus modules, duration, and pricing.
- **Mentors (`MentorsPage.jsx`)**: Biographies, years of experience, and specialties of trade mentors (customs brokers, maritime captains, LC legal advisors).
- **Resources (`ResourcesPage.jsx`)**: Free downloadable guides, Incoterms matrix, trade checklists, field articles, and video masterclass previews.
- **Contact Us (`ContactPage.jsx`)**: Enquiries form, phone, email, operating hours, and office address.
- **Login (`LoginPage.jsx`)**: Student portal login for existing users with account access and registration link.

## 🚀 How to Run

1. Start development server:
   ```bash
   npm run dev
   ```
2. Open **`http://localhost:5173`** in your browser.
3. Build for production:
   ```bash
   npm run build
   ```
